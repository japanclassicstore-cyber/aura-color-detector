# 💜 Aura Color Detector

**Discover Your Energy Color & Unlock Your Spiritual Potential**

A mystical aura reading application that reveals your unique energy color based on your personality and lifestyle. Built with **Claude 4.5 Sonnet** (code) and **xAI Grok** (images).

## ✨ Features

- 🔮 **Aura Color Quiz** - 8 personality questions reveal your energy
- 🎨 **6 Aura Results** - Purple, Blue, Green, Gold, Pink, Red
- ✨ **Animated Aura Glow** - Visual representation of your energy
- 🔮 **Crystal Ball Animation** - Mystical reading experience
- 📱 **Social Sharing** - Share your aura on Twitter/Facebook
- 🌟 **Floating Particles** - Ethereal background animation
- 📱 **Mobile Responsive** - Works on all devices

## 🚀 Technologies

- **AI Code Generation:** Claude 4.5 Sonnet (via Segmind API)
- **AI Image Generation:** xAI Grok API
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (Vanilla)
- **Design:** Glassmorphism, Purple/Pink/Gold palette
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display, Lato (Google Fonts)
- **Hosting:** GitHub Pages
